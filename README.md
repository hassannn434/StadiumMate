# 🏟️ StadiumMate
### Your Match. Your Seat. Your Experience.

**StadiumMate** is a stadium matchday companion prototype designed to make the live sports experience more convenient by allowing fans to browse and order food, beverages, and team merchandise through a QR-based, seat-oriented ordering experience.

## 🚀 Live Demo

🔗 **[Explore StadiumMate – Live Application](https://tangerine-semifreddo-409529.netlify.app/)**

## 📌 Project Overview

Watching a live match should be about enjoying every moment. However, fans often miss important match action when they leave their seats to purchase refreshments or merchandise.

StadiumMate explores a convenient digital ordering experience that helps fans interact with stadium services while staying connected to the game.

## ✨ Key Features

- **QR-Based Access:** Scan a QR code to access the stadium ordering experience.
- **Food Ordering:** Browse available food options.
- **Beverage Ordering:** Explore beverage options.
- **Team Merchandise:** Access a dedicated merchandise category.
- **Seat-Oriented Ordering:** Designed around the fan's stadium seat.
- **Order Tracking:** Follow the order status through the application.
- **Interactive Interface:** Simple and user-friendly matchday experience.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. A fan enters the stadium.
2. The fan scans the QR code associated with their seat.
3. StadiumMate opens the ordering interface.
4. The fan explores food, beverages, or team merchandise.
5. The fan places an order.
6. The application provides an order-tracking experience.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional stadium purchasing experiences can require fans to leave their seats, stand in queues, and miss important moments during live matches.

StadiumMate explores a digital-first approach to make stadium services more accessible and convenient.

## 💡 Proposed Impact

- Improve the convenience of stadium purchases.
- Reduce the need for fans to leave their seats.
- Enhance the overall matchday experience.
- Create opportunities for a more connected stadium ecosystem.

## 🔮 Future Enhancements
